CRAFTSMANSHIP & ENGINEERING

Materials & Fabrication Mastery

We offer four distinct fabrication methods tailored to your structural constraints, design language, and desired installation timeline.

Manual / Custom-Made

Furniture hand-built on-site by experienced master carpenters to exact millimeter site dimensions and personalized architectural specs.

Best for: Irregular alcoves, solid teak accents, bespoke curved paneling.

Semi-Modular

A smart hybrid pairing factory-manufactured standardized carcass boxes with on-site custom carpenter trims, fillers, and end-panels.

Best for: Balancing budget with factory edge finish on non-orthogonal walls.
Popular

Modular (Factory Precision)

100% factory-manufactured modules with PUR edge-banding, pre-drilled CNC fittings, rapid dust-free on-site assembly, and high longevity.

Best for: Sleek kitchens, sliding wardrobes, acrylic finishes, speedy handover.

Imported European

Luxury cabinetry, German & Italian motion hardware (Blum, Grass, Salice), lacquered glass, and rare international surface finishes.

Best for: Ultra-luxury villas, motorized pocket doors, connoisseur aesthetics.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding our pricing models, timelines, or warranty? Everything you need to know before embarking on your interior journey.

Our pricing is structured transparently per square foot across three distinct tiers: Standard (₹1,000–₹2,000/sq.ft), Luxury (₹1,200–₹2,000/sq.ft), and Ultra-Luxury (₹2,000–₹5,000/sq.ft). Once 3D designs and bill-of-quantities (BOQ) are signed off, our contract price is locked. There are zero unexpected cost escalations unless you add new architectural scope.

A standard 2,000 to 3,500 sq. ft. luxury apartment typically takes 45 to 60 working days from design sign-off. Larger independent villas ranging from 4,000 to 10,000 sq. ft. involving extensive double-height civil work and imported stone finishes require 75 to 90 working days. We assign dedicated site engineers to guarantee milestone compliance.

Manual Carpentry is built by hand on-site, making it ideal for non-standard wall angles, custom decorative fluting, and solid teak items. Factory Modular involves automated CNC cutting and high-temperature edge-banding in a precision facility, delivering flawless waterproof edges, zero on-site dust, and rapid assembly. We also offer Semi-Modular, which thoughtfully combines both worlds.

Yes, absolutely. We have extensive on-site experience across Hyderabad’s top gated enclaves (including My Home, Rajapushpa, Aparna, EIPL, and GHM). We strictly comply with society working hours, debris disposal rules, service elevator protections, and noise restriction mandates.

We provide up to a 10-year warranty on modular hardware (Hafele, Hettich, Blum) and boiling water resistant marine plywood against delamination and borer attacks. Additionally, we provide 1-year complimentary on-site maintenance support for hinge realignment, touch-ups, and soft-close calibrations.
